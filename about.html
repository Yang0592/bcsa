About Better Care SA

Better Care SA is a South Australian provider of disability and aged care supports. We focus on safe, person-centred support that respects people’s choices, culture and everyday routines.

Our Story

Better Care SA was created by health professionals and support workers who saw how much everyday details matter – the timing of medication, the way you like your meals, the support you need to feel calm and in control.

We bring experience from nursing, mental health, disability support and aged care. Our goal is to turn this experience into practical, reliable support for people living in South Australia.

Our Work

We partner with NDIS participants, older persons, families and support coordinators to design support that fits real life – not just the paperwork.

  • In-home disability and aged care support
  • Short and medium term accommodation options
  • Supported independent living and daily living skills
  • Social and community participation support

Our Values

Our values guide how we recruit, train and support our team, and how we show up in people’s homes and communities.

Respect

We respect each person’s culture, identity, preferences and life story, and we protect their right to make decisions about their own support.

Empathy

We listen carefully, notice the small changes and respond with patience and genuine care – especially on harder days.

Accountability

We do what we say we’ll do. We welcome feedback and use it to keep improving our services, policies and practice.

Why Choose Better Care SA?

  • Person-centred planning and clear communication
  • Experienced team with clinical and community backgrounds
  • Flexible support times, including evenings and weekends
  • Strong focus on safety, dignity and independence

Care that grows. Support that lasts.

Our name reflects our promise – to grow with you as your goals and needs change, and to provide support that feels steady and dependable over time.
