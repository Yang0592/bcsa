Work With Us

We’re building a small, values-driven team who care about getting the details right – from how we speak to people, to how we document supports and communicate with families and coordinators.

Who we’re looking for

We welcome applications from support workers, enrolled and registered nurses, and others with experience in disability or aged care who:

  • Value respect, empathy and accountability
  • Are comfortable working in people’s homes and communities
  • Communicate clearly and kindly, even on busy days
  • Are reliable and committed to safe, quality practice

What we offer

  • Person-centred, values-based approach to support
  • Clear policies, procedures and guidance
  • Supportive supervision and opportunities to learn
  • Flexible shifts where possible, depending on participant needs

How to express interest

If you’d like to work with Better Care SA, please send us a brief introduction and your resume. Let us know:

  • Your current qualifications and registrations
  • Your experience in disability, mental health or aged care
  • The types of shifts or locations you’re interested in
  • Why you’d like to be part of Better Care SA

You can email your information to info@bettercaresa.com or use the contact form on our website.
