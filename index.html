Supporting people across South Australia

We work alongside NDIS participants, older persons, families and support coordinators to design supports that feel safe, respectful and manageable day to day.

NDIS participants

In-home and community supports tailored to your NDIS goals, routines, preferences and cultural needs.

Older persons & carers

Practical, gentle support to stay safe and comfortable at home, while giving family and carers a break.

Families & coordinators

Clear communication, reliable rostering and transparent documentation to make your role easier.

How we can help

Our team brings experience from nursing, mental health, disability support and aged care into everyday, practical supports.

In-home support

Assistance with personal care, meals, medication prompts, light domestic tasks and daily routines.

Community participation

Support to get to appointments, social activities, hobbies, shopping and community events.

Short & medium term stays

Flexible short stays to support respite, recovery or transitions between living arrangements.

Skill building

Support to build skills in daily living, communication, planning, budgeting and community access.

Overnight & complex support

Planned and reliable support for higher needs and overnight routines, depending on availability.

Partnering with coordinators

Responsive communication, progress updates and collaboration with support coordinators and providers.

Ready to talk about support?

Whether you’re just starting with the NDIS, reviewing your plan or exploring new support options, we’re happy to listen and help you plan next steps.
