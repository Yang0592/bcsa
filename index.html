About Better Care SA

Better Care SA is a disability and aged care support provider based in South Australia. We work alongside people with disability, older persons and their families to design support that fits real life – not the other way around.

Our team brings experience in nursing, mental health, disability support and aged care. We focus on safety, dignity and meaningful choice in everyday life.

  • In-home support and community participation
  • Short and medium term accommodation options
  • Person-centred planning and goal setting
  • Respectful, trauma-informed practice

Mission, Vision & Values

We exist to make everyday life safer, calmer and more meaningful for the people we support.

Our Mission

To deliver person-centred support that protects people’s rights, promotes health and wellbeing, and builds independence at each person’s own pace.

Our Vision

A community where people with disability and older persons feel safe, heard and empowered to make decisions about their own lives – every day.

Our Values

  • Respect
  • Empathy
  • Accountability
  • Collaboration

These values guide how we recruit, how we train and how we show up in people’s homes.

Our Services

Flexible support options that can be combined to match your NDIS plan, aged care package and personal goals.

In-Home Support

Personal care, daily living support, medication prompts and light household tasks so you can live safely and comfortably at home.

Social & Community Participation

Support to attend appointments, groups, hobbies and social events, building confidence and community connections.

Short Term Accommodation (Respite)

Short stays in a supportive environment that offer a break for you and your carers, while keeping routines as familiar as possible.

Medium Term Accommodation

Safe, fully supported accommodation while you wait for a long-term housing solution or home modifications.

Supported Independent Living

24/7 shared or individual living arrangements with support to develop skills and maintain independence at home.

Transport Assistance

Practical support to get to appointments, work, day programs and social activities using safe and reliable transport.
