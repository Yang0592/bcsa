Our Services

Better Care SA offers flexible disability and aged care supports that can be matched to your NDIS plan, aged care package and personal goals. We can support you at home, in the community and in short or longer term accommodation.

Social & Community Participation

Support to build and maintain social connections, attend activities, groups and events, and stay involved in your community.

Learn more

Short Term Accommodation (Respite)

Short stays in a supportive environment that give you and your informal supports a break, while keeping routines as familiar as possible.

Learn more

Medium Term Accommodation (MTA)

Safe, accessible accommodation while you wait for a long-term housing solution or home modifications to be completed.

Learn more

Supported Independent Living (SIL)

24/7 shared or individual living arrangements with support to manage daily tasks, build skills and live as independently as possible.

Learn more

In-Home Support

Personal care, daily living support, meal preparation and light domestic assistance in your own home – at times that suit you.

Learn more

Transport Assistance

Assistance to attend appointments, work, education and social activities, using safe, reliable and accessible transport options.

Learn more

We can walk through your current supports and plans, then suggest a combination of services that match your goals and budget.
