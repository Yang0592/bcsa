Our Services

Better Care SA provides flexible supports for NDIS participants and older persons living in South Australia. We aim to make everyday life safer, calmer and more manageable – at home and in the community.

What we can support with

The exact supports depend on your goals, funding and what feels most helpful for you and your family.

In-home daily living support

Help with personal care, showering, dressing, grooming, meals, light domestic tasks and routines that keep you comfortable and safe at home.

  • Personal care
  • Meals & nutrition
  • Domestic assistance

Community access & appointments

Support to attend appointments, shopping, social activities, therapies and community events, with a focus on confidence and safety.

  • Medical appointments
  • Social outings
  • Hobbies & interests

Short & medium term accommodation

Stays that support respite, recovery or transitions between living arrangements, with planned routines and clear communication with families and coordinators.

  • Respite support
  • Transition stays
  • Planned, person-centred care

Skill building & independence

Support to build everyday skills such as cooking, planning, communication, budgeting and travel, at a pace that feels safe for you.

  • Daily living skills
  • Household tasks
  • Confidence building

Support for older persons

Gentle, respectful support for older people living at home, with a focus on safety, dignity and reducing stress for carers.

  • Companionship
  • Routine support
  • Carer relief

Working with coordinators & providers

We partner with support coordinators, plan managers and other providers to deliver joined-up support and clear documentation.

  • Progress updates
  • Goal-focused reporting
  • Collaborative planning

NDIS & aged care funding

We provide services to NDIS participants and older persons who are self-managed or plan-managed. We can also work with you if you are using private funding while exploring government-funded options.

During your first conversation with us, we will clarify what supports are possible under your funding and talk through the most important priorities for you and your family.

Not sure where to start?

You don’t need to have all the answers before you call. We can help you work out what might be most useful right now and what to consider in the future.
